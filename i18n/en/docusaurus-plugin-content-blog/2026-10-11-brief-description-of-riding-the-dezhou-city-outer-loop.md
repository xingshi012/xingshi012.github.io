After getting up early, washing up, and having breakfast, I set off on today's ride around Dezhou's outer ring road.
<!-- truncate -->

During the National Day holiday, I had originally planned to cycle to a different county town around Dezhou each day. A few things at work got in the way, though, so I changed my plans and mapped out a ride around Dezhou's outer ring road instead.

The loop runs around Dezhou's central urban area, taking in the East Outer Ring Road (G513), the North Outer Ring Road, Yunhe Avenue (also known as the West Outer Ring Road), and S516 (the South Outer Ring Road). This ride left quite an impression on me, perhaps because it was my first time cycling more than 70 km. I was exhausted and my legs ached. It also felt quite unsafe: much of the outer ring follows national highways, with heavy trucks roaring past one after another. I treated it as a personal challenge to see whether I could complete the loop. Fortunately, I made it through without any physical problems beyond the fatigue. It gave me a better idea of my abilities, which should help me plan future rides. From now on, I'll try to choose city streets or scenic routes around reservoirs, lakes, and rivers. Those roads tend to be safer and have less motor traffic. This ride certainly taught me what it feels like to share a national highway with heavy trucks. Even with earphones in, I could barely hear the music playing on my phone over the constant roar of passing trucks.

Here's a closer look at the route. I started on the East Outer Ring Road and then joined the North Outer Ring Road. Those first two sections felt fairly uneventful, since I was still fresh, and I stopped to take quite a few photos along the way. Things changed at one of the route's checkpoints. I had planned the ride in a map app that required me to pass each waypoint before it would move on to the next section. At an intersection on the North Outer Ring Road, however, the app failed to register my arrival. I'm not sure whether my phone's location was inaccurate or I had placed the waypoint incorrectly when planning the route. After that, I was mostly riding without navigation. I remembered that I needed to head to the western end of the North Outer Ring Road, leave it, and turn south. At Yuzhuang Interchange, though, I took the wrong branch at a fork between leaving the national highway and joining the expressway. I nearly reached the expressway toll plaza, then rode back against traffic for about a hundred meters to return to the junction and leave the North Outer Ring Road. From there, I headed south along National Highway 105 and west along Fumaying Road until I reached the West Outer Ring Road, or Yunhe Avenue. It was almost noon by then. I went a little farther north, crossed into Hebei Province, and stopped for a large bowl of Shanxi-style wide noodles. They were pretty good.

Then I began the ride south. At this point, I really needed navigation if I wanted to find my way home, so I switched to another app and plotted a route south toward Huangzhuang Interchange. This was probably the toughest stretch of the whole ride. Broken glass and loose gravel covered the non-motorized lane, the road was narrow, and there were plenty of heavy trucks—every difficulty seemed to pile up at once. Near Huangzhuang Interchange, the second navigation app ran into trouble too. Yunhe Avenue is also a national highway, with a barrier dividing the road. The app told me to turn east, but the barrier made that impossible. I kept heading south and, at Huangzhuang Interchange, followed the navigation onto rural roads. After a somewhat confusing series of turns south and then east, I found myself riding east along a country road parallel to the South Outer Ring Road. That stretch was pleasant: little traffic and a smooth surface. Eventually, I turned a little north and reached an intersection with the South Outer Ring Road. I followed it east, turned north onto the East Outer Ring Road, and finally returned to its intersection with Dongfanghong East Road, completing the loop.

It was a new challenge and a useful reality check. For future rides, I'll try to avoid roads with heavy trucks. On narrow roads with so many large vehicles, you never quite know what you might encounter. During this ride, I saw plenty of broken glass on the North Outer Ring Road and Yunhe Avenue, pieces of car debris on the South Outer Ring Road, and even corn being dried right in the non-motorized lane on the East Outer Ring Road. I'm glad I reached the finish safely and without incident. The full ride covered 77.37 km in 5 hours, 31 minutes, and 18 seconds, at an average speed of 14 km/h. I'd say that's a decent result for my first ride of this length.
